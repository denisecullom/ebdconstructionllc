How much does a home remodel cost in Gainesville, GA?

Typical Gainesville-area ranges are about $8,000 to $70,000 for a mid-range kitchen remodel, $5,000 to $30,000 for a hall bathroom, $15,000 to $90,000 for a single room addition, $20,000 to $75,000 for a finished basement and $40,000 to $250,000 for a full whole-home renovation. Smaller refreshes start around $1,500 to $2,000.
