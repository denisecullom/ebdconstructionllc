How Much Does a Laundry Room or Mudroom Remodel Cost in Gainesville, GA?

Costs for laundry refreshes, built-in cabinets, drop zones and benches, moving the washer and dryer, and adding a mudroom, plus the details that keep the space dry and working, for homeowners in Gainesville, Hall County and around Lake Lanier.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · November 7, 2026

Quick answer: In Gainesville and Hall County, a laundry room refresh typically costs about $1,500 to $6,000, and a full laundry remodel with cabinets, counters, tile and a utility sink about $5,000 to $20,000. A mudroom drop zone with a bench, cubbies and hooks runs about $2,000 to $8,000, a full mudroom remodel about $6,000 to $25,000, and moving the laundry to another floor about $5,000 to $15,000. What makes a laundry room work better?

  • A folding counter over front-loaders, or beside top-loaders, at about 36 inches high.
  • Hanging space. A rod or pull-out drying rack for clothes that can't go in the dryer.
  • A utility sink for stains, muddy cleats and paint brushes.
  • A short, straight dryer vent. Long, crushed or plastic vents are a fire risk and make drying take longer. We use smooth metal duct to the outside.
  • Leak protection. A drain pan, braided supply hoses and an automatic shutoff valve, especially on an upper floor.
  • Good light. LED ceiling lights plus under-cabinet lights over the counter.

What makes a good mudroom?

  • One locker or cubby per person, about 15 to 24 inches wide, with a hook for a bag and a coat.
  • A bench at about 18 inches high with shoe storage underneath.
  • Upper cabinets or baskets for hats, gloves and off-season gear.
  • A hard, waterproof floor. Porcelain tile, or luxury vinyl plank on a budget. See our flooring guide.
  • A charging spot and mail drop so phones and paper don't land on the kitchen counter.
  • A pet station. A dog wash or a feeding nook is popular with Hall County families.

Should I move the laundry to the main floor or upstairs?

Many older Gainesville homes have the washer and dryer in a basement, garage or carport, which means hauling baskets up and down stairs. Moving it near the bedrooms or to the main level is one of the most popular changes in a ranch or split-level remodel, and it's a must for aging in place. A closet next to a bathroom is often the easiest spot, because the water and drain lines are already close. Expect about $5,000 to $15,000, and plan for a drain pan and shutoff valve on any upper floor.

Where can I find room for a mudroom?

  • The garage entry. Borrowing a few feet of the garage is the most common and least expensive option.
  • A back hall or breakfast nook near the most-used door.
  • A covered back porch. Enclosing part of it gives you a mudroom without a new foundation.
  • A small addition. About $15,000 to $50,000; see our home additions guide.

Do I need a permit?

Cabinets, shelves, paint and a new floor usually don't. Moving the washer or dryer, adding a sink, new circuits, a gas line, a new dryer vent or any addition needs permits and inspections in Gainesville and Hall County. Eric pulls all permits himself; see our building permits guide.

Laundry room and mudroom FAQs

How much does it cost to move a laundry room upstairs?

Moving a washer and dryer to a second floor or the main level typically costs about $5,000 to $15,000. That covers new water lines, a drain and vent, a 240-volt dryer circuit or gas line, a dryer vent to the outside, a drain pan and often some framing and flooring. Living near the bedrooms is worth it for many families.

What is the best flooring for a laundry room or mudroom?

Porcelain tile is the best choice for most laundry rooms and mudrooms because it's waterproof and nearly indestructible. Luxury vinyl plank is a good, warmer, lower-cost option. Avoid solid hardwood and carpet in either room.

Do I need a permit to remodel a laundry room?

A cosmetic refresh with paint, shelves and cabinets usually doesn't. Moving the washer or dryer, adding a sink, new circuits, a gas line or a new dryer vent needs plumbing and electrical permits and inspections. Eric pulls all permits himself.

Can I add a mudroom to my house?

Yes. Many Gainesville homes turn part of a garage, a back hall or a covered porch into a mudroom, which costs far less than an addition. A small mudroom addition at the back or garage door typically costs about $15,000 to $50,000 including the foundation, roof and finishes.

How long does a laundry or mudroom remodel take?

A refresh or built-in drop zone takes about 3 days to 1 week. A full laundry or mudroom remodel takes about 1 to 3 weeks, moving the laundry about 1 to 2 weeks, and a mudroom addition about 6 to 12 weeks, plus time for cabinets and permits.
