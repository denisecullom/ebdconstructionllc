How Much Does It Cost to Finish a Bonus Room or Attic in Gainesville, GA?

Costs, code requirements and options for turning an unfinished room over the garage or an attic into a bedroom, office, playroom or guest suite in Gainesville, Flowery Branch, Oakwood, Lake Lanier and Hall County.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · October 23, 2026

Quick answer: Finishing an existing bonus room over the garage in Gainesville and Hall County usually costs $15,000 to $45,000, or about $50 to $150 per square foot, when the floor, stairs and roof framing are already there. Converting a plain attic that needs a stronger floor, permanent stairs or a dormer usually runs $35,000 to $90,000+. Adding a bathroom adds about $15,000 to $40,000. It's one of the least expensive ways to add a bedroom, office or playroom, because the roof, walls and foundation already exist. Can my attic or bonus room be finished?

Many Gainesville-area homes built since the 1990s were built with a "bonus room over garage" already framed and only waiting for finishes. Others have a big attic that may or may not work. Here's what decides it:

  • Ceiling height. Georgia's residential code, based on the International Residential Code, requires a habitable room to have a ceiling of at least 7 feet over at least half of its floor area, and space under 5 feet tall doesn't count as floor area. A quick test: stand in the middle of the attic. If there's a stretch at least 7 feet tall and about 7 feet wide running most of its length, it's worth a closer look.
  • Roof framing. Rafters, or "attic trusses" built with an open room inside, are good candidates. Standard trusses with W-shaped webs filling the attic can't be cut without an engineer's design and major rework, which usually makes the project too expensive.
  • Floor strength. Attic floors are often framed only to hold insulation and light storage. Living space needs a floor built for people and furniture, so the joists may need to be added to or replaced.
  • Stairs. A pull-down ladder doesn't count. Living space needs a permanent stair, generally at least 36 inches wide, with risers no taller than 7¾ inches, treads at least 10 inches deep and 6 feet 8 inches of headroom. Finding room for it is often the hardest part of an attic conversion.

Can a bonus room be a bedroom?

Yes, if it meets the rules for a sleeping room. That means at least 70 square feet and 7 feet in each direction, enough ceiling height, heat and air, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and an emergency escape window: an opening of about 5.7 square feet, at least 24 inches tall and 20 inches wide, with the sill no more than 44 inches above the floor. Many bonus rooms only have a small gable window or skylights, so a larger window or a dormer is often part of the job. Adding a closet makes the room count as a bedroom for most buyers and appraisers.

How do you keep a bonus room comfortable?

The number one complaint about rooms over the garage is that they're hot in summer and cold in winter. The fixes are insulation and the right heating and cooling:

  • Insulate everything around the room. The floor over the garage, the knee walls, the sloped ceiling and the small attic spaces behind the knee walls all need to be sealed and insulated. Spray foam at the roofline is often the best answer for sloped ceilings.
  • Choose the right HVAC. A ductless mini-split gives the room its own thermostat and is usually the most comfortable choice. Extending the existing system's ducts costs less but only works if it has spare capacity, and the room may still run warmer than the rest of the house.
  • Ventilate the roof. The roof above still needs air flow or properly installed foam, or the shingles and sheathing can trap heat and moisture. Our roof guide covers ventilation.

What can a finished bonus room or attic be used for?

  • Extra bedroom. With a closet and egress window, it adds a true bedroom.
  • Home office. Away from the kitchen and the TV, and quieter than a garage conversion.
  • Playroom or teen hangout. Toys and noise stay upstairs.
  • Guest or in-law suite. Add a bathroom and it becomes a private space for visitors or family.
  • Media or game room. Sloped ceilings and few windows make a cozy theater.

Do I need a permit to finish a bonus room or attic?

Yes. Turning unfinished space into living space generally needs a building permit in Gainesville and unincorporated Hall County, plus electrical, mechanical and plumbing permits for wiring, HVAC and a bathroom. Finishing without a permit can cause problems when you sell, because the room may not count as living space and buyers' inspectors often catch it. Eric pulls every permit himself. See our permits guide for fees and timelines. If you live in an HOA, interior work usually doesn't need approval, but a new dormer or window on the outside usually does.

Bonus room and attic FAQs

Can any attic be turned into a room?

No. A finished room needs at least about 7 feet of ceiling height over at least half of its floor area, a floor strong enough for living space, and permanent stairs. Attics built with W-shaped trusses usually cannot be converted without major structural work, while rafter-framed roofs and attic trusses designed with a room inside are good candidates.

Can a bonus room be a legal bedroom?

Yes, if it meets the code for a sleeping room: at least 70 square feet and 7 feet wide, enough ceiling height, heating and cooling, smoke and carbon monoxide alarms, and an emergency escape window with an opening of about 5.7 square feet that is no more than 44 inches above the floor.

How do you heat and cool a bonus room over a garage?

A ductless mini-split, about $3,000 to $8,000, is often the best fix for a room over a garage that is too hot or cold. Extending the existing ductwork costs less, about $1,500 to $5,000, but only works if the system has extra capacity. Good insulation in the floor over the garage, the knee walls and the roofline matters as much as the equipment.

Do I need a permit to finish a bonus room in Hall County?

Yes. Turning unfinished space into living space generally needs a building permit, plus electrical, mechanical and plumbing permits as needed, in both Gainesville and unincorporated Hall County. EBD Construction pulls every permit itself.

How long does it take to finish a bonus room?

Finishing an existing bonus room usually takes about 3 to 6 weeks once permits are approved. Attic conversions with new stairs, floor reinforcement or dormers usually take about 6 to 12 weeks.

Does a finished bonus room add value to a home?

Usually yes. A finished, heated and cooled room reached by permanent stairs is typically counted as living space when a home is appraised or sold, and an extra bedroom or office is one of the most requested features. It costs far less per square foot than building an addition.
