Do I need a permit to convert a garage in Hall County?

Yes. Turning a garage into a bedroom, office or suite changes it from storage to living space, so it needs a building permit with inspections for framing, electrical, plumbing and insulation. Homes in Gainesville city limits are permitted through the City of Gainesville and homes in unincorporated Hall County through Hall County; Flowery Branch, Oakwood and Buford handle their own. Zoning matters too: some areas and HOAs limit a second kitchen or renting the space to a tenant, and a few require covered parking. EBD Construction pulls all required permits itself and checks these rules before you pay for a design.

What does a garage need to become a real room?

A garage is built for cars, not people, so a good conversion fixes a few things:

  • The floor. Garage slabs usually sit a step lower than the house and slope toward the door. We level it with sleepers and a new subfloor, or raise it to meet the house, and add a vapor barrier so Georgia ground moisture doesn't come up through the flooring.
  • Insulation. Exterior walls, the ceiling and the new wall where the door was all get insulated to the same standard as the rest of the house.
  • Light and egress. A bedroom needs a window big enough to climb out of in an emergency. We usually put it in the new wall where the garage door was.
  • Heating and cooling. Most conversions use a ductless mini-split.
  • The outside. Siding, trim and windows that match the house so it doesn't look like a closed-in garage. The driveway can stay, or be cut back and landscaped.

Can you add a bathroom to a garage conversion?

Yes. The garage slab has to be cut for the drain lines, which run to your main sewer or septic line. If the slab is too low for the drain to flow, an up-flush or ejector system solves it. A half bath typically starts around $6,000; a full bath with a walk-in shower costs more. For a parent moving in, we often build the bathroom as an aging-in-place bath with a low-threshold shower and grab bars from day one. See the bathroom remodeling guide for fixtures and tile.

Is a garage conversion cheaper than an addition?

Usually, yes, often by half or more. A room addition needs a new foundation, walls and roof, so it typically costs $75 to $300 per square foot in the Gainesville area. A garage already has all three. The trade-off is losing parking and storage. If you have a walk-out basement, finishing it is the other low-cost way to add a bedroom or suite.

Most conversions take about 3 to 8 weeks of work once permits are issued. An in-law suite with a bathroom and kitchenette can take 8 to 12 weeks. A typical EBD conversion goes like this:

Garage conversion FAQs

Can you keep part of the garage?

Yes. On a two-car garage, a common plan is to convert one bay into a room and keep the other for a car or storage, with a new insulated wall between them.

Can I rent out a converted garage?

Maybe. Renting a separate unit depends on your zoning and HOA. Eric checks the rules for your address before design starts.

Do I need to remove the garage door?

Usually. Most conversions replace the door with a framed, insulated wall and a window or entry door, with siding to match the house. That looks better and makes the room far more comfortable.

Can you convert a detached garage?

Yes. A detached garage can become an office, studio or guest suite. Running power, water and sewer to it costs more than an attached garage, so it's worth pricing early.

Will my converted garage stay cool in a Georgia summer?

Yes, if it's insulated properly and has its own heating and cooling. A ductless mini-split sized for the room keeps it comfortable year-round.
