What are the most popular remodeling projects in south Hall County homes?

A lot of homes in Flowery Branch, Oakwood, Chestnut Mountain and Buford were built from the 1990s through the 2010s. They're solid houses with a few common upgrades on homeowners' lists:

  • Updating builder-grade kitchens with new counters, painted or new cabinets, an island and better lighting.
  • Swapping a garden tub for a walk-in shower in the primary bath.
  • Screening in a deck or adding a screened porch, mostly to escape mosquitoes and spring pollen.
  • Opening the kitchen to the living room by taking out a wall.
  • Finishing a walk-out basement for a family room, guest suite or office.
  • Replacing a worn-out deck on lake-area homes near Lake Lanier.

Do I need a permit to remodel in Flowery Branch, Oakwood or Buford?

Most remodels that change walls, plumbing, electrical, structure or the outside of the house need a building permit. Which office issues it depends on your address: homes inside city limits may go through the City of Flowery Branch, the City of Oakwood or the City of Buford, while homes in unincorporated south Hall go through Hall County. Buford and Braselton also cross into Gwinnett, Jackson and Barrow counties, each with its own building department. Eric checks which office covers your address and pulls all required permits himself, then schedules the inspections, so you don't have to sort it out.

Does my HOA need to approve my remodel in Flowery Branch or Buford?

Often, yes. Many neighborhoods in Flowery Branch, Oakwood, Buford and Braselton have a homeowners association with an architectural review committee. Interior remodels usually don't need HOA approval, but porches, decks, additions, siding colors, roofs and anything visible from the street usually do. EBD can provide drawings, materials and color samples for your HOA application, and the work is scheduled once approval comes back.
