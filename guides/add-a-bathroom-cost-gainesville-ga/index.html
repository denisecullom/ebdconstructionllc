How Much Does It Cost to Add a Bathroom in Gainesville, GA?

Costs for adding a half bath, a full bath in existing space, a basement bathroom or a bathroom addition, plus where a new bathroom can go and what drives the price, for homeowners in Gainesville, Hall County and around Lake Lanier.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · November 9, 2026

Quick answer: In Gainesville and Hall County, adding a half bath or powder room in existing space typically costs about $8,000 to $25,000. A full bathroom carved out of existing space runs about $15,000 to $45,000, a basement bathroom with rough-ins about $6,000 to $30,000, and a bathroom addition about $35,000 to $90,000+. The biggest factor is how far the new bathroom is from existing plumbing. Where can a new bathroom go?

  • Next to existing plumbing. Back-to-back with a bathroom, kitchen or laundry is the cheapest spot by far.
  • A hall or bedroom closet. A deep closet is often just big enough for a half bath.
  • Under the stairs. A classic spot for a powder room if there's enough headroom over the toilet.
  • Part of a large bedroom. Taking 5 by 8 feet from a big bedroom can create a private bath for that room.
  • The basement. Many Hall County basements already have rough-ins. See our basement finishing guide.
  • A garage, bonus room or addition. When there's no spare room inside. See our home additions guide.

How much space does a bathroom need?

BathroomMinimum practical size
Half bath (toilet and sink)About 18–20 sq ft, such as 3 x 6 or 4 x 5 feet
Three-quarter bath (adds a shower)About 30–36 sq ft, such as 5 x 6 or 6 x 6 feet
Full bath (tub or tub-shower)About 40 sq ft, such as 5 x 8 feet

Code requires at least 15 inches from the center of the toilet to a side wall or fixture, and 21 inches of clear floor in front of the toilet and sink. A shower must be at least 30 by 30 inches inside.

What about septic systems?

Many homes around Gainesville, Lake Lanier and north Hall County are on septic. Adding a half or full bath doesn't always change the system, but adding a bedroom with it, or a system that's already near capacity, can require a review by Hall County Environmental Health and sometimes a larger tank or field. Eric checks this before you commit to a plan.

Do I need a permit?

Yes. A new bathroom needs a building permit with plumbing, electrical and framing inspections in Gainesville and unincorporated Hall County. Eric pulls all permits himself and schedules every inspection; see our building permits guide.

Is adding a bathroom worth it?

Usually, yes. A three-bedroom house with one bathroom is one of the hardest homes to sell, and a second full bath is one of the first things Hall County buyers ask about. Nationally, a bathroom addition recoups roughly half its cost at resale, and the payoff is best when a home has fewer bathrooms than bedrooms. See our remodeling ROI guide.

Adding a bathroom FAQs

How much space do you need for a half bath or full bath?

A half bath with a toilet and sink can fit in about 18 to 20 square feet, such as a 3-by-6 or 4-by-5 foot space. A three-quarter bath with a shower needs about 30 to 36 square feet, and a full bath with a tub usually needs about 40 square feet, such as 5 by 8 feet. Code requires at least 15 inches from the center of the toilet to a wall and 21 inches of clear space in front of it.

What is the cheapest place to add a bathroom?

The cheapest spot is right next to or directly above or below an existing bathroom or kitchen, because the new fixtures can tie into nearby water, drain and vent lines. Closets, the space under a stair, a corner of a large bedroom or a laundry room are common spots.

Can you add a bathroom in a basement below the sewer line?

Yes. If the basement floor sits below the main sewer line, an ejector pump or an up-flush system lifts waste up to the line. That usually adds about $1,500 to $5,000. If the slab has no rough-ins, cutting the concrete to run drains typically adds $2,000 to $6,000.

Does adding a bathroom require a permit in Hall County?

Yes. A new bathroom needs a building permit with plumbing and electrical inspections in Gainesville and unincorporated Hall County. Homes on septic may need Environmental Health to review the system's capacity. Eric pulls all permits himself.

How long does it take to add a bathroom?

A half bath in existing space usually takes about 2 to 4 weeks of work, and a full bath about 4 to 8 weeks. A bathroom addition with a new foundation and roof typically takes 3 to 5 months including permits.

Does adding a bathroom add value to a home?

Usually, yes. A house with only one bathroom is harder to sell, and a second full bath is one of the features Hall County buyers ask for most. Nationally, a bathroom addition recoups roughly half its cost at resale, and the payoff is highest in homes with fewer bathrooms than bedrooms.
