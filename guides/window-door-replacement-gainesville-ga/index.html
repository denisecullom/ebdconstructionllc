How Much Does Window and Door Replacement Cost in Gainesville, GA?

Costs for replacement windows, whole-house window projects, new or larger openings, and entry, patio and French doors, plus signs it's time and how to choose, for homeowners in Gainesville, Hall County and around Lake Lanier.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · November 8, 2026

Quick answer: In Gainesville and Hall County, a vinyl replacement window typically costs about $500 to $1,200 installed, fiberglass about $900 to $2,000, and wood or clad-wood about $1,000 to $2,500+. Replacing 15 to 20 windows runs about $8,000 to $25,000 in vinyl. A new entry door costs about $1,500 to $6,000, a patio door $2,000 to $6,000, and a French door $3,000 to $10,000. How do I know it's time to replace windows or doors?

  • Fog between the panes. The seal has failed and the glass has lost its insulating gas.
  • Drafts, sticking or windows that won't stay up. Common in 1970s to 1990s Gainesville homes.
  • Soft wood or peeling paint at the sill or bottom of the door frame, a sign water is getting in.
  • Single-pane glass or aluminum frames. They sweat in winter and let in summer heat.
  • High summer power bills and rooms that stay hot on the west and south sides.
  • A dated front entry that drags down curb appeal.

Insert or full-frame replacement?

An insert window slides into the existing frame. It's faster, cheaper and leaves the trim alone, and it works well when the old frame is solid and square. A full-frame replacement takes the window out to the studs, so we can fix hidden rot, install new flashing tape and pan flashing, and change the size. If you're also replacing siding, full-frame is the right call. See our siding and exterior remodeling guide.

What windows are best for North Georgia?

  • Low-E glass with a low solar heat gain coefficient, about 0.25 or less, to block summer heat.
  • ENERGY STAR certification for the southern climate zones.
  • Double-pane is plenty for Gainesville's climate; triple-pane rarely pays back here.
  • Egress size for bedrooms: an opening of at least 5.7 square feet (5.0 on the ground floor), at least 24 inches high and 20 inches wide, with a sill no more than 44 inches off the floor.
  • Tempered glass near doors, tubs, showers and stairs, as code requires.

Is there a tax credit for new windows?

Not anymore at the federal level: the energy-efficient home improvement credit for windows and doors ended for products installed after December 31, 2025. Check with your tax professional, and ask Georgia Power or your electric cooperative about any current rebates. For ways to pay for a bigger exterior project, see how to pay for a remodel.

Do I need a permit?

Like-for-like window and door replacements usually don't need a building permit in Gainesville or Hall County. New or enlarged openings, structural changes and work tied to an addition or porch do. Homes built before 1978 need EPA lead-safe practices when painted surfaces are disturbed. Eric pulls all permits himself; see our building permits guide.

Window and door replacement FAQs

How much does it cost to replace an exterior door?

A steel or fiberglass entry door typically costs about $1,500 to $6,000 installed with trim and hardware. A sliding patio door runs about $2,000 to $6,000, a French door about $3,000 to $10,000, and a storm door about $400 to $1,200.

What is the difference between insert and full-frame replacement windows?

An insert window fits inside the existing frame, so the inside and outside trim stay put. It's faster and cheaper. A full-frame replacement removes the old window down to the rough opening, which lets us fix rot, add flashing and change the size, and usually adds about $300 to $1,000 per window.

What are the best replacement windows for Georgia?

For most Gainesville-area homes, double-pane vinyl or fiberglass windows with a low-E coating rated for the Southeast hold up well and cut heat gain in summer. Look for ENERGY STAR certification for the South-Central or Southern climate zone and a low solar heat gain coefficient.

Do I need a permit to replace windows in Hall County?

Replacing windows and doors the same size usually doesn't need a building permit, but making an opening bigger, adding a new window or door, or changing structure does. Homes built before 1978 need lead-safe work practices. Eric pulls all permits himself.

Can I add a new window or make one bigger?

Yes. Adding or enlarging a window typically costs about $1,500 to $5,000 on top of the window itself, for the new header, framing, siding, trim, drywall and paint. Load-bearing walls need a properly sized header, and bedroom windows have to meet egress size.

Is there a tax credit for new windows and doors?

The federal tax credit for energy-efficient windows and doors ended for products installed after December 31, 2025. Check with your tax professional and with Georgia Power or your electric cooperative for any current rebates.
