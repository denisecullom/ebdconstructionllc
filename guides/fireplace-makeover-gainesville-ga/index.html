How Much Does a Fireplace Makeover Cost in Gainesville, GA?

From painted brick and a new mantel to floor-to-ceiling stone, built-ins and gas inserts: prices, timelines and what to check first, for homes in Gainesville, Hall County and around Lake Lanier.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · October 28, 2026

Quick answer: A fireplace makeover in Gainesville and Hall County usually costs $300 to $1,500 to paint or limewash brick, $500 to $3,000 for a new mantel, $3,000 to $12,000 for stone veneer refacing, and $8,000 to $30,000 for a full floor-to-ceiling makeover with a new mantel and built-ins. A gas insert adds $3,000 to $7,000. Most projects take a few days, and a full makeover with built-ins about 2 to 4 weeks. The biggest cost drivers are how tall the wall is (vaulted great rooms take a lot more stone), the material you choose, and whether the firebox, hearth or chimney need work.

What are the most popular fireplace makeover ideas?

  • Painted or limewashed brick. The fastest, lowest-cost change. Limewash gives a soft, aged look and lets some brick show through.
  • A chunky wood beam mantel. A rough-sawn or stained beam warms up a painted or stone fireplace and is one of the most requested updates.
  • Floor-to-ceiling stone. Stacked stone or a rustic ledgestone up to the ceiling turns the fireplace into the focal point of the room, especially in vaulted Lake Lanier and mountain-style homes.
  • Tile or a slab surround. Large-format tile, a quartz slab or zellige tile around the firebox for a clean, modern look.
  • Shiplap or paneling. Painted shiplap or board-and-batten with a simple mantel for a farmhouse or coastal look. Combustible materials have to stay the required distance from the firebox opening.
  • Built-ins. Cabinets below and open shelves above on both sides, often with lighting, for storage and a finished look.
  • A TV above the fireplace. Recessed wiring, a hidden outlet and a mount set at the right height. Check the heat under the mantel first.

Tip: Have the chimney inspected before you spend money on the look. A cracked flue liner or a leaking chimney crown is much easier to fix before the new stone or mantel goes on.

How long does a fireplace makeover take?

Paint and a new mantel take a day or two. Tile, shiplap or stone refacing takes about 3 to 7 days, mostly waiting on mortar and grout to set. A full makeover with built-ins usually takes 2 to 4 weeks, because the cabinets are built, installed and painted. Gas inserts often take a couple of weeks to order. Many homeowners schedule the work in late summer or early fall so the fireplace is ready for the first cold night. See our best time to remodel guide.

Do I need a permit?

Paint, tile, stone veneer, a new mantel and built-ins are cosmetic and usually don't need a permit in Gainesville or Hall County. Installing a gas insert or a new gas line, adding a new fireplace, or structural work on the chimney usually does. Clearances between the firebox and anything combustible, like a wood mantel or shiplap, are set by code and by the fireplace manufacturer. Eric pulls any permits himself. Fireplace makeover FAQs

Can you put stone over an existing brick fireplace?

Usually, yes. Manufactured stone veneer can go over clean, sound brick with a scratch coat of mortar, or over a framed and sheathed chimney chase with a moisture barrier and metal lath. It saves the cost of tearing the brick out.

How much does it cost to add built-ins next to a fireplace?

Built-in cabinets with shelves on both sides of a fireplace usually cost about $3,000 to $15,000, depending on the size, whether they're painted or stained, and whether they include lighting or doors.

Should I convert my wood-burning fireplace to gas?

Many Gainesville homeowners do. A gas insert costs about $3,000 to $7,000 installed and heats far better than an open fireplace; vented gas logs cost about $800 to $3,000 and are mostly for looks. You'll need a gas line, and the chimney should be inspected first.

How long does a fireplace makeover take?

Painting brick or a new mantel takes 1 to 2 days. A stone or tile refacing takes about 3 to 7 days, and a full makeover with built-ins about 2 to 4 weeks, including cabinet building and paint.

Do I need a permit for a fireplace makeover?

Cosmetic work like paint, tile, stone veneer and a new mantel usually does not need a permit. Installing a gas insert or new gas line, a new fireplace, or structural work on the chimney usually does. Eric pulls any permits himself.

Can you remove a fireplace and chimney?

Yes. Removing a fireplace and chimney usually costs about $3,000 to $15,000 or more, depending on whether it is brick or a framed chase, how tall it is, and the roof, floor and wall patching afterward.
