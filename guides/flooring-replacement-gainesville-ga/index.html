How Much Does New Flooring Cost in Gainesville, GA?

What LVP, hardwood, tile and refinishing cost, how long each takes, and which floor fits your house, for homes in Gainesville, Flowery Branch, Oakwood, Lake Lanier and across Hall County.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · October 19, 2026

Quick answer: Installed flooring around Gainesville typically costs about $3 to $9 per square foot for luxury vinyl plank (LVP) or laminate, $7 to $15 for engineered hardwood, $8 to $18 for solid hardwood and $8 to $20 for porcelain tile, labor included. That works out to roughly $800 to $5,000 for one room, $4,000 to $25,000 for an open main level and $8,000 to $55,000 or more for a whole house. Refinishing existing hardwood is often the cheapest upgrade at $1,500 to $8,000. Most main-level jobs take three to seven days. Which flooring is best for a Gainesville home?

North Georgia's humid summers, red clay that tracks in on every shoe, and lots of crawl-space and basement homes all shape what works. Here's how the common choices hold up:

  • Luxury vinyl plank (LVP). The most popular choice we see right now. It's waterproof, stands up to dogs and kids, looks like wood and can go in every room, including kitchens, baths and basements. Buy a thick wear layer (20 mil or more) for busy households. It can't be refinished, and cheap LVP can look like plastic.
  • Engineered hardwood. Real wood on top of a plywood core. It moves less with humidity than solid wood, so it's a safer choice over concrete slabs, basements and crawl spaces. Thicker wear layers can be sanded and refinished once or twice.
  • Solid hardwood. The classic for living rooms, dining rooms and halls. It can be sanded and refinished many times and lasts the life of the house. It dents more easily and doesn't belong in baths or below grade.
  • Tile. Porcelain tile is the best floor for bathrooms, laundry rooms and mudrooms. It's waterproof and nearly indestructible, but it's cold and hard underfoot, and it needs a stiff subfloor or it will crack.
  • Laminate. Cheaper than LVP and more scratch-resistant, but most laminates don't like standing water, so keep them out of baths and laundry rooms.
  • Carpet. Still the most comfortable and cheapest choice for bedrooms and bonus rooms, but most buyers now want hard floors in living areas.

Should I choose luxury vinyl plank or hardwood?

Choose LVP if you have pets, young kids or a basement, want one floor through the kitchen and baths, or are on a tighter budget. Choose hardwood if you're planning to stay a long time, want a floor that can be refinished instead of replaced, or are in a price range where buyers expect real wood. A lot of Hall County homeowners split the difference: hardwood in the living, dining and bedrooms, and LVP or tile in the kitchen, baths, laundry and basement.

Should I refinish or replace my hardwood floors?

If you already have solid hardwood under that carpet or under a worn finish, refinishing is often the best money you can spend on a house. The floor is sanded to bare wood, stained if you want a new color, and coated with fresh finish, for about $3 to $6 per square foot.

Refinish when the boards are solid and tight and the damage is surface scratches, gray worn paths or an orange 1990s color. Replace when boards are cupped, buckled or black from water, when they move or squeak badly underfoot, when the wood has been sanded so many times the nails show, or when it's a thin engineered floor with no wear layer left. Small damaged areas can often be patched with matching boards before refinishing.

What problems can show up under the old floor?

This is where hiring a general contractor instead of a flooring store pays off. Once the old floor is up, we often find:

  • Soft or rotted subfloor around toilets, tubs, dishwashers, refrigerator ice makers and exterior doors.
  • Moisture from the crawl space. Many older Gainesville homes sit over damp crawl spaces, which cups wood floors and grows mold. A vapor barrier or dehumidifier may need to come first.
  • Dips and humps from settled framing or a poured slab that isn't flat. Floating floors and tile both need a flat surface.
  • Squeaks and bounce from loose subfloor or undersized joists, which we can screw down or stiffen while the floor is open.

Because Eric's crew does carpentry, tile, trim and drywall, these get fixed the same week instead of stopping the job while you hunt for another contractor.

How long does a flooring project take?

  1. Consultation and measuring: Eric walks the house, checks the subfloor and moisture, and measures. You get a written estimate.
  2. Ordering: stocked LVP, laminate and tile can be ready in days; special-order wood or tile takes about 1 to 3 weeks. Hardwood is delivered early and sits in the house for 3 to 7 days to adjust to the humidity.
  3. Tear-out and prep: old flooring comes out, subfloor is repaired and leveled, usually 1 to 2 days.
  4. Installation: a room takes 1 to 2 days, a main level 3 to 7 days, a whole house 1 to 3 weeks. Tile needs an extra day for grout.
  5. Trim and cleanup: baseboards, shoe molding, transitions and stair noses go on, doors are trimmed if needed, and furniture goes back.

Can I replace carpeted stairs with wood?

Yes, and it's one of the most requested parts of a flooring job. The carpet and pad come off, the old rough treads are cut flush or removed, and new hardwood treads and painted risers go on, usually $2,000 to $8,000 for a standard 13 to 15 step staircase. It's also the right time to replace dated spindles with iron balusters and a new handrail.

Do I need a permit for new flooring?

Replacing floor coverings usually doesn't need a permit in Gainesville or unincorporated Hall County. Replacing rotted subfloor or joists, or flooring that's part of a bigger remodel with walls, plumbing or electrical changes, may. Eric pulls every permit himself. Flooring FAQs

What is the best flooring for a house in Georgia?

For most Gainesville-area homes, waterproof luxury vinyl plank is the best value: it handles humidity, red clay, pets and kids, and it can go in kitchens, baths and basements. Engineered hardwood is the best choice if you want real wood, because it moves less than solid wood in Georgia's humid summers. Porcelain tile is the best choice for bathrooms, laundry rooms and mudrooms.

How long does it take to install new floors?

A single room usually takes one to two days. An open main level takes about three to seven days, and a whole house one to three weeks. Hardwood often needs to sit in the house for three to seven days first so it can adjust to the humidity, and special-order materials can take one to three weeks to arrive.

Can new flooring go over my old floor?

Sometimes. Floating vinyl plank or laminate can often go over flat, sound vinyl or tile. Carpet always comes out, and anything soft, uneven or water-damaged should come out so the problem underneath can be fixed. Going over an old floor also raises the height, which can cause trouble at doors, stairs and appliances.

Can I stay in my house while new floors are installed?

Usually, yes. Floors are done in sections so you can keep using part of the house. Furniture is moved room by room. Hardwood refinishing is the exception: you will need to stay off the floors for one to three days while the finish dries, and some finishes have a strong smell.

Does new flooring add value to a home?

Yes. Worn carpet and mismatched floors are among the first things buyers notice, and refinished or new hardwood is one of the most requested features in Gainesville-area listings. Matching floors through the main level make a house look bigger and more updated in listing photos.
