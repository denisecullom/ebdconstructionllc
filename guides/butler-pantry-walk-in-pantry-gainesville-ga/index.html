Walk-in pantry, butler's pantry or pantry cabinets?

  • Pantry cabinet wall: best when there's no spare room. Tall cabinets with roll-out shelves hold a surprising amount and look built-in.
  • Walk-in pantry: best for bulk food, small appliances and paper goods, with everything visible on open shelves. It needs a closet-sized space next to the kitchen.
  • Butler's pantry: best for people who entertain. A counter for coffee, drinks or serving keeps the main kitchen clear, and a beverage fridge keeps guests out of the cook's way.
  • Scullery: a butler's pantry with a sink and sometimes a second dishwasher, so dirty dishes and countertop appliances stay hidden from an open-plan living room.

What makes a pantry work well?

  • Shallow shelves. 12 to 16 inches deep for food, so nothing hides in the back; deeper only for small appliances.
  • Adjustable or varied heights, with a counter-height shelf for the mixer, air fryer or coffee maker.
  • Outlets and lighting. Plugs at counter height and an LED light on a door switch or motion sensor.
  • A 3-foot aisle in a walk-in pantry, and 36 to 42 inches in front of base cabinets.
  • Ventilation if appliances will live there, and a solid surface that can take spills.

Pantry FAQs

What is the difference between a butler's pantry and a walk-in pantry?

A walk-in pantry is a small room or large closet lined with shelves for food and small appliances. A butler's pantry is a pass-through or side space between the kitchen and dining room with cabinets and a countertop, often with a sink, wine or beverage fridge, coffee station or extra dishwasher, for serving and staging. A scullery is a working butler's pantry where the messy prep and dishes happen out of sight.

How big does a walk-in pantry need to be?

A walk-in pantry works in as little as about 4 by 5 feet with shelves on two or three sides. A 3-foot clear aisle lets one person turn around comfortably, and 12 to 16 inches deep is the most useful shelf depth for food. A butler's pantry needs about a 36- to 42-inch aisle in front of base cabinets.

Where can I find space for a pantry?

Common spots are a nearby coat or hall closet, part of a laundry room or mudroom, the space under the stairs, a little-used desk nook, a corner of a large dining room, or part of a garage behind the kitchen wall. If there's no space inside, a small bump-out addition can add a pantry and a bigger kitchen at the same time.

How long does it take to build a pantry?

Shelving a closet or a walk-in pantry usually takes 3 days to 2 weeks. A butler's pantry with new cabinets and countertops usually takes 3 to 6 weeks on site, plus 4 to 10 weeks to get semi-custom or custom cabinets. Adding plumbing for a sink adds a few days.

Do I need a permit to add a pantry?

Shelves and cabinets alone usually don't need a permit. Moving or adding walls, adding outlets or lighting circuits, or running plumbing for a sink usually does in Gainesville and Hall County. Eric Bubniak pulls all permits himself.
