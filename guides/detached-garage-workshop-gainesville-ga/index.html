How Much Does a Detached Garage or Workshop Cost in Gainesville, GA?

Costs for building a one- or two-car detached garage, a workshop or studio, a carport or a garage with an apartment above, plus site work, permits and setbacks, for homeowners in Gainesville, Hall County and around Lake Lanier.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · November 10, 2026

Quick answer: In Gainesville and Hall County, a stick-built one-car detached garage typically costs about $25,000 to $50,000 and a two-car garage about $40,000 to $90,000, including the slab, framing, roof, siding, garage doors and basic power. A garage with a finished workshop or bonus loft runs about $60,000 to $150,000+, and a garage apartment about $120,000 to $250,000+. That's roughly $60 to $120 per square foot for a basic garage. Stick-built, metal or prefab?

  • Stick-built (what we build). Framed on site to match your house's roof pitch, siding and trim. Lasts for decades, adds the most value and can hold a loft or apartment later.
  • Metal kit buildings. Cheaper per square foot and quick to put up, but often look out of place next to a house and can be restricted by HOAs.
  • Prefab sheds and garages. Fine for small storage, but sizes and finishes are limited and most can't hold a finished workshop.

What should a workshop have?

  • Enough power. A 60- to 100-amp subpanel with 240-volt outlets for a table saw, compressor, welder or EV charger.
  • Good light. LED shop lights plus windows or skylights.
  • Insulation and a mini-split. North Georgia summers make an uninsulated shop hard to use from June to September.
  • A wide door. A 9- or 10-foot overhead door or double doors to move equipment, a boat or a mower.
  • A thicker slab where heavy tools, a lift or a boat will sit.

Do I need a permit, and what are the setbacks?

Yes. A detached garage, workshop or garage apartment needs a building permit in Gainesville and unincorporated Hall County, with footing, framing, electrical and final inspections. Small sheds under about 200 square feet are often exempt from a building permit, but every accessory building still has to meet the zoning setbacks from property lines, height limits and any HOA rules. A garage apartment also has to be allowed by your zoning, and septic homes may need Environmental Health review. Eric checks all of this up front and pulls all permits himself; see our building permits guide.

How long does it take?

Most detached garages take about 6 to 12 weeks to build once the permit is issued: a few days for site work and the slab, one to two weeks for framing and roofing, then siding, doors, power and trim. Permits and HOA approval usually add 2 to 6 weeks before work starts. Fall and winter are good times to start; see the best time to remodel.

Detached garage and workshop FAQs

What does a detached garage cost per square foot?

A basic detached garage typically runs about $60 to $120 per square foot around Gainesville. A garage with insulation, drywall, a mini-split and finished workshop space runs about $120 to $250 or more per square foot, and a garage apartment costs about the same per foot as a small addition.

Do I need a permit to build a detached garage in Hall County?

Yes. A detached garage, workshop or garage apartment needs a building permit with footing, framing, electrical and final inspections. It also has to meet zoning setbacks from property lines, and many subdivisions have HOA rules on size, height and materials. Eric pulls all permits himself.

How long does it take to build a detached garage?

Most detached garages take about 6 to 12 weeks to build once the permit is issued, depending on size, site work and weather. Permits and HOA approval usually add 2 to 6 weeks before work starts.

Is it cheaper to build an attached or detached garage?

A detached garage is often about the same cost or a little cheaper, because it doesn't have to tie into your roof, foundation or siding. An attached garage saves on a fourth wall and is closer to the house, but it means opening the house and matching the roofline. Lot size and setbacks usually decide it.

Can I build a garage with an apartment above it?

Often, yes. A garage apartment typically costs about $120,000 to $250,000 or more around Gainesville. Zoning decides whether a second dwelling is allowed on your lot, and homes on septic may need Environmental Health approval. See our in-law suite guide for every option.

How much does a carport cost?

An open carport with a roof that matches the house typically costs about $8,000 to $25,000 around Gainesville, depending on size and whether it attaches to the house.
