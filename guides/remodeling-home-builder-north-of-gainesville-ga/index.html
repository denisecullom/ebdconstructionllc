Can I build a house on my land in north Hall, White or Lumpkin County?

Usually, yes. Building on land you already own is one of the most common projects north of Gainesville. Before plans are drawn, Eric walks the property with you to look at:

  • Access: how long the driveway will be and whether trucks and equipment can reach the house site.
  • Slope: whether the site suits a slab, a crawlspace or a walk-out basement.
  • Water and sewer: most rural lots need a well and a septic system, and the septic permit depends on a soil test.
  • Power: how far the nearest line is and what the utility will charge to bring it in.
  • Zoning and setbacks: what the county allows on your parcel.

If you haven't bought land yet, Eric can look at a lot with you before you buy, so you know what site work will cost.

What should I know about remodeling an older farmhouse or cabin?

Older homes in the foothills often have charm worth keeping and a few things worth checking first. Common issues EBD looks for:

  • Floor framing and crawlspaces that need sistering, new supports or moisture control.
  • Old wiring and plumbing that should be updated while the walls are open.
  • Porches and decks with rotted posts, ledgers or railings that need rebuilding.
  • Small, closed-off rooms that can be opened up by removing a wall.
  • Too little space, solved with a room addition or a screened porch or sunroom.

For bigger projects, see the whole-home renovation guide, which also covers whether to renovate or move.

Who issues building permits north of Gainesville?

It depends on the county and town. North Hall homes (Murrayville, Clermont, Lula, Gillsville) are permitted through Hall County unless they're inside a city's limits. White County covers Cleveland and the area around it, Lumpkin County covers Dahlonega, and Habersham County covers Demorest and Cornelia, with some towns running their own building offices. Septic permits come from each county's Environmental Health office. Eric checks which office covers your property and pulls all required permits himself.

How long does building or remodeling take north of Gainesville?

  • New custom home: about 8 to 14 months of building after permits, plus 1 to 4 months of planning.
  • Addition: about 2 to 4 months.
  • Kitchen or bath remodel: about 2 to 10 weeks.
  • Deck or porch: about 1 to 6 weeks.

Well drilling, septic approval and long driveways can add time at the start of a new build, so it pays to get those moving early.

Building and remodeling north of Gainesville FAQs

Do you travel to Dahlonega, Cleveland and Demorest?

Yes. EBD Construction is based in Gainesville and builds and remodels in Murrayville, Clermont, Lula, Gillsville, Cleveland, Dahlonega, Demorest and Cornelia.

Can you build a cabin or small home on my land?

Yes. Cabins, guest cottages and smaller custom homes are a good fit for family land and acreage. Eric will walk the property with you first to check access, slope, water and septic.

Do I need a well and septic system?

Most rural lots north of Gainesville don't have public water or sewer, so a well and septic system are common. The septic permit depends on a soil test done through the county Environmental Health office.

Can you build a home with a walk-out basement on a sloped lot?

Yes. Sloped lots are common in the foothills and often suit a walk-out basement, which adds living space and a ground-level entry on the low side.

Do you remodel older farmhouses?

Yes. EBD updates older homes with new kitchens, baths, open layouts, porches and additions, and checks framing, wiring and plumbing along the way.

Is EBD Construction licensed and insured?

Yes. EBD Construction LLC is licensed and insured, all work is guaranteed, and references are available on request.
