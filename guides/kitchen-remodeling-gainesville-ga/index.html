What does a kitchen remodel include?

A full kitchen remodel with EBD Construction typically includes:

  1. Layout and design. Moving or adding an island, opening a wall to the living room, or reworking the "work triangle" so the kitchen fits how you cook and gather.
  2. Custom cabinetry. Including paneled appliances that blend into the cabinets for a built-in look.
  3. Countertops. Granite and quartz, plus backsplash tile.
  4. Lighting. Recessed lights, pendants over the island and under-cabinet lighting.
  5. Flooring. Tile, hardwood or luxury vinyl plank that ties into the rest of the home.
  6. The work behind the walls. Plumbing, electrical and drywall, handled by the same team so nothing falls through the cracks.
  7. Wall removal and open-concept work. EBD also handles wall removal and ceiling work for homeowners who want an open kitchen and living area.

Not every kitchen needs everything on this list. If you only want part of it, like new countertops and a backsplash, say so during your free consultation and the plan will be built around that.

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Gainesville, GA?

The cost of a kitchen remodel in Gainesville depends mostly on four things: the size of the kitchen, whether the layout changes, the cabinet and countertop materials you choose, and how much plumbing and electrical has to move.

Here's a general guide to what kitchen remodels cost in the Gainesville area. It isn't a quote; your price depends on your kitchen and your choices.

Tip: Keeping your sink, range and refrigerator in the same spots is one of the biggest ways to save, because moving plumbing and gas lines adds labor and materials.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Most kitchen remodels in the Gainesville area take about 6 to 12 weeks from demolition to final walkthrough. Demolition and rough-in plumbing and electrical usually take 1 to 2 weeks, and the rest is drywall, cabinets, countertops, tile and finishes. Custom cabinets can take 8 to 12 weeks to build, so they're usually ordered before demolition starts to keep the time your kitchen is out of service as short as possible.

A typical EBD kitchen project follows these steps:

  1. Free consultation. Eric visits your home, listens to what you want and looks at the space.
  2. Design and estimate. You get a clear plan, a written estimate and a timeline.
  3. Selections and ordering. You choose cabinets, countertops, tile, fixtures and lighting.
  4. Permits. Eric pulls every permit himself, with the City of Gainesville or Hall County depending on where you live, so you don't have to deal with the paperwork.
  5. Demolition and rough-in. The old kitchen comes out; plumbing and electrical are updated.
  6. Build-out. Drywall, cabinets, countertops, backsplash, flooring and lighting go in.
  7. Final walkthrough. You walk the finished kitchen with Eric and anything that isn't right gets fixed.

Do I need a permit to remodel my kitchen in Gainesville or Hall County?

Usually, yes, if the project involves electrical, plumbing, gas, or structural work such as removing a wall. Cosmetic work like painting or replacing a countertop in the same spot often does not need a permit. Homes inside Gainesville city limits are permitted through the City of Gainesville; homes in unincorporated Hall County go through Hall County. Flowery Branch, Oakwood and Buford each have their own process. EBD Construction pulls all required permits itself as part of the project, so you don't have to.

Kitchen remodeling FAQs

What is the best time of year to remodel a kitchen in Georgia?

Any time of year works for an indoor kitchen remodel. Many Gainesville homeowners plan in late winter or spring so the kitchen is done before summer gatherings, or start in late summer to finish before the holidays. Booking early gives you the most choice of start dates.

Can I live in my house during a kitchen remodel?

Yes, most homeowners stay in their home. EBD keeps the work area contained and cleans up daily. Many families set up a temporary kitchen with a microwave, coffee maker and refrigerator in another room.

Do you help with design, or do I need a designer?

EBD designs and builds custom kitchen layouts, so you do not need to hire a separate designer. If you already work with a designer or architect, EBD can build from their plans.

Is quartz or granite better for a kitchen countertop?

Both are durable and popular in Gainesville homes. Quartz is non-porous and doesn't need sealing, so it's lower maintenance. Granite is natural stone with unique patterns and handles heat well, but it should be sealed periodically. EBD installs both.

Can you remove a wall to open my kitchen to the living room?

Often, yes. EBD handles wall removal and ceiling work to create open living spaces. Whether a wall can be removed, and what it takes, depends on whether it's load-bearing and what runs through it. That's assessed during the free consultation.
