What is the best siding for Georgia homes?

North Georgia is hard on siding: hot, humid summers, heavy rain, termites, carpenter bees and woodpeckers. Here's how the common options compare:

MaterialProsThings to know
Fiber cement (like James Hardie)Resists rot, termites, woodpeckers and fire; holds paint well; looks like woodCosts more than vinyl; heavier to install; needs paint every 10 to 15 years
VinylLowest cost; no painting; easy to cleanCan crack or fade; looks less like wood up close
Engineered wood (like LP SmartSide)Real-wood look; lighter than fiber cement; treated against rot and termitesEdges and cuts must be sealed and painted well
Natural wood or cedarClassic, warm look; great for accents and shakesNeeds the most upkeep in humid weather; attracts carpenter bees

Many Gainesville homes mix materials: fiber cement or vinyl on most of the house, with board-and-batten or shake accents in gables and stone at the base. Eric can help you choose what fits your house, your budget and your neighborhood.

How do I know if my siding needs to be replaced?

  • Soft or rotting boards, especially near the ground, along roof lines and under windows and doors.
  • Paint that keeps peeling a year or two after it was painted.
  • Warped, cracked or loose pieces, or gaps where siding meets trim.
  • Holes from carpenter bees or woodpeckers, which usually means the wood is soft.
  • Water stains, musty smells or mold on inside walls.

A few bad boards can be repaired. When the damage is on several sides of the house, or the siding is past its life, replacing it costs less over time than chasing repairs.

Siding and exterior FAQs

Can I replace just part of my siding?

Yes. Rotten boards near the ground, under windows or along roof lines can usually be replaced on their own. The tricky part is matching older siding profiles and colors, so a fresh coat of paint often follows.

Should I paint or replace my siding?

If the siding is solid and only the paint is failing, painting is the lower-cost choice. If boards are soft, rotting or keep peeling, painting just hides the problem; replacing the damaged siding, or the whole wall, lasts much longer.

Can you put new siding over old siding?

It's sometimes done, but EBD usually recommends removing old siding so any rotten sheathing, framing or missing house wrap can be found and fixed before the new siding goes on.

What color siding is popular in Gainesville right now?

Grays, greige, deep blue-greens, warm whites and dark charcoal with white or black trim are all popular, often with board-and-batten or shake accents and stone at the base.

Does new siding help with energy bills?

It can. Replacing siding is a good time to add house wrap and seal gaps, and insulated siding or foam board can be added for extra protection.

What else can improve curb appeal?

New front doors, shutters, porch columns, railings, a covered entry, painted trim, retaining walls and steps all make a big difference, often for much less than a full residing.
