How Much Does an Outdoor Kitchen Cost in Gainesville, GA?

Costs for grill stations, full outdoor kitchens, patios, fire pits and outdoor fireplaces, plus what to build on and what to put under a roof, for homeowners in Gainesville, Hall County and around Lake Lanier.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · November 5, 2026

Quick answer: In Gainesville and Hall County, a built-in grill station typically costs about $3,000 to $10,000, a mid-size outdoor kitchen with a grill, counters, sink and refrigerator about $10,000 to $30,000, and a full covered outdoor kitchen about $25,000 to $75,000+. A new paver patio runs about $15 to $35 per square foot, and a fire pit $1,000 to $5,000. Where should the outdoor kitchen go?

  • Close to the indoor kitchen. Fewer trips with plates and food. Just off the back door or deck stairs is ideal.
  • On a solid base. A patio or concrete pad is simplest. A deck can carry a kitchen if it's framed for the weight; see our deck repair or replace guide if yours is older.
  • Smoke away from the house. Keep the grill out from under low eaves and away from windows, and follow the grill maker's clearances to walls and railings.
  • Shade for summer. A west-facing patio in Gainesville gets brutal afternoon sun. A roof or pergola makes it usable in July.
  • Near utilities. The shorter the gas, water and power runs, the lower the cost.

Should I put a roof over it?

Usually, yes. A roof protects the grill, counters and fridge from sun, rain and pollen, and it lets you cook through a summer storm. You can add a shed or gable roof over a new or existing patio, or build the kitchen under a covered deck or patio roof. If you want bugs out too, consider a screened porch with the grill on a patio just outside. A vent hood is recommended when a grill sits under a roof.

What materials hold up in North Georgia?

  • Frame: steel studs or concrete block, never wood, under stone or stucco.
  • Finish: natural or veneer stone, brick or stucco to match the house.
  • Counters: granite is a great outdoor choice; poured concrete and porcelain slab also work. Most quartz fades in direct sun. See our countertops guide.
  • Doors and appliances: 304-grade stainless steel holds up to humidity best.
  • Patio: pavers or textured concrete for grip when wet.

Do I need a permit?

A freestanding grill on a patio doesn't. A new gas line, electrical circuits, sink plumbing, a roof or pavilion and an outdoor fireplace usually do, in both the City of Gainesville and unincorporated Hall County. HOAs often want to review plans too. Eric pulls all permits himself; see our building permits guide.

Outdoor kitchen and patio FAQs

Can an outdoor kitchen go on a wood deck?

Yes, if the deck is framed for the weight. Stone counters, a grill and a refrigerator can weigh well over a ton, so the deck may need extra joists, beams and footings under the kitchen, plus a non-combustible surface and clearances around the grill. On older decks, a patio or a concrete pad beside the deck is often the better base.

Is a paver patio or concrete better for an outdoor kitchen?

Both work. Poured concrete is usually cheaper, about $8 to $18 per square foot, and makes a flat, solid base. Pavers cost more, about $15 to $35 per square foot, but look better, flex with Georgia's clay soil and can be repaired one piece at a time.

Do I need a permit for an outdoor kitchen in Hall County?

Usually for the parts that matter: a new gas line, electrical circuits and outlets, plumbing for a sink, a roof or pavilion, and an outdoor fireplace typically need permits and inspections. A freestanding grill on a patio does not. Eric pulls all permits himself.

Should an outdoor kitchen be covered?

In North Georgia, a roof makes an outdoor kitchen usable far more often, through summer sun, pop-up storms and pollen season, and it protects appliances and counters. A roof adds about $8,000 to $40,000 depending on size and style.

What materials last best for an outdoor kitchen in Georgia?

Stone, brick or stucco over a steel or masonry frame, granite or concrete counters, and 304-grade stainless steel appliances and doors hold up best to humidity, heat and pollen. Avoid wood frames and indoor cabinets.

How long does it take to build an outdoor kitchen?

A grill station or patio takes about 1 to 2 weeks. A full outdoor kitchen takes about 2 to 4 weeks, and 4 to 8 weeks with a roof or pavilion, plus time for permits and appliance delivery.
