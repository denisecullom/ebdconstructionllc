What does a bathroom remodel include?

A full bathroom remodel with EBD Construction can include:

  1. Layout and design. Moving the toilet or vanity, enlarging the shower, or turning a closet or unused corner into more bathroom.
  2. Showers. Walk-in and curbless showers with frameless glass, rain heads, benches and built-in niches.
  3. Tubs. Freestanding soaking tubs, tiled drop-in tubs, or a tub-to-shower conversion if you'd rather have the space.
  4. Tile. Floors, shower walls, accent walls and mosaic details, set over proper waterproofing.
  5. Vanities and storage. Single or double vanities with stone or quartz tops, plus built-in linen towers.
  6. Lighting and fixtures. Recessed lights, sconces, chandeliers, faucets, mirrors and an exhaust fan sized for the room.
  7. The work behind the walls. Plumbing, electrical, framing and drywall, handled by the same crew.

Not every bathroom needs everything on this list. If you only want a new vanity and a tiled shower, say so during your free consultation and the plan will be built around that.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Gainesville, GA?

The cost of a bathroom remodel in Gainesville depends mostly on four things: the size of the bathroom, whether the plumbing moves, the tile and fixtures you choose, and whether you keep the tub or build a custom shower.

Here's a general guide to what bathroom remodels typically cost in the Gainesville area. It isn't a quote; your price depends on your bathroom and your choices.

Project typeWhat it usually involvesTypical range
Bathroom refreshNew vanity, toilet, faucets, lighting, mirror and paint; same layout$1,500 to $12,000
Tub-to-shower conversionOld tub out, new tiled walk-in shower with glass door, valve and niche$3,000 to $18,000
Full hall or guest bath remodelDown to the studs: new tile, tub or shower, vanity, lighting, plumbing and electrical updates$5,000 to $30,000
Primary (master) bath remodelLarger layout changes, walk-in or curbless shower, soaking tub, double vanity, custom tile$10,000+

The fastest way to get a real number is a free on-site consultation. EBD works within your budget and will tell you up front where your money goes furthest.

Tip: Keeping your toilet, sink and shower drain where they are now is one of the biggest ways to save. Moving drains means opening floors and adds labor. Putting your splurge into one feature, like a tiled shower or a statement vanity, gets you the most impact per dollar.

How long does a bathroom remodel take?

Most bathroom remodels in the Gainesville area take about 3 to 5 weeks for a hall or guest bath and 6 to 10 weeks for a primary bath with layout changes. A simple refresh or a tub-to-shower conversion can often be done in 1 to 2 weeks. Tile work and waterproofing take the most time, because each layer needs to cure before the next goes on. Custom vanities and glass shower doors are measured and ordered during the job, so they're planned into the schedule from the start.

A typical EBD bathroom project follows these steps:

  1. Free consultation. Eric visits your home, listens to what you want and looks at the space.
  2. Design and estimate. You get a clear plan, a written estimate and a timeline.
  3. Selections and ordering. You choose tile, vanity, countertop, fixtures, glass and lighting.
  4. Permits. Eric pulls every permit himself, with the City of Gainesville or Hall County depending on where you live.
  5. Demolition and rough-in. The old bathroom comes out; framing, plumbing, electrical and ventilation are updated and inspected.
  6. Waterproofing and tile. Shower pans and walls are waterproofed, then floors and walls are tiled.
  7. Finishes. Vanity, countertop, fixtures, glass, lighting and paint go in.
  8. Final walkthrough. You walk the finished bathroom with Eric and anything that isn't right gets fixed.

Do I need a permit to remodel my bathroom in Gainesville or Hall County?

Usually, yes, if the project involves plumbing, electrical or structural work: moving a drain or toilet, adding circuits or lighting, installing an exhaust fan, or taking out a wall. Swapping a vanity, faucet or toilet in the same spot, or painting and changing hardware, often does not need a permit. Homes inside Gainesville city limits are permitted through the City of Gainesville; homes in unincorporated Hall County go through Hall County. Flowery Branch, Oakwood and Buford each have their own process. EBD Construction pulls all required permits itself as part of the project, so you don't have to.

Should I replace my tub with a walk-in shower?

It depends on who uses the bathroom. In a primary bath, many Gainesville homeowners trade a tub they rarely use for a larger walk-in or curbless shower that's easier to step into and easier to live with as they get older. In a hall bath used by kids or guests, keeping at least one tub in the house is usually a good idea, both for bathing young children and for resale. EBD can talk through both options with you during the consultation.

Bathroom remodeling FAQs

Can I use my bathroom during a remodel?

Not the one being remodeled, for most of the job. If it's your only full bath, tell Eric during the consultation. The schedule can be planned so the toilet is out of service for as short a time as possible, and a tub-to-shower conversion or refresh can often be finished in 1 to 2 weeks.

What is a curbless shower, and can my bathroom have one?

A curbless shower has no step or curb at the entrance, so the bathroom floor runs straight into the shower. It looks open and is easy to walk into, which makes it a good choice for aging in place. It usually requires recessing the floor where the shower sits, which is assessed during the free consultation.

What tile is best for a bathroom?

Porcelain tile is the most popular choice for floors and shower walls because it's dense, durable and comes in marble, stone and wood looks. Natural marble is beautiful but needs sealing. Small mosaic tile is a good choice for shower floors because the extra grout lines add grip. EBD installs all of them.

Do you help with design, or do I need a designer?

EBD designs and builds bathroom layouts, so you do not need to hire a separate designer. If you already work with a designer or architect, EBD can build from their plans.

Can you add a bathroom where there isn't one now?

Often, yes. EBD builds new bathrooms in basements, additions and converted spaces. What it takes depends on where water and drain lines can run, which is assessed during the free consultation.
