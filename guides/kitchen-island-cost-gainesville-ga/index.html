How Much Does It Cost to Add a Kitchen Island in Gainesville, GA?

Costs for simple and custom islands, islands with a sink or cooktop, and peninsulas, plus the size, seating and walkway space you need, for homeowners in Gainesville, Hall County and around Lake Lanier.

By Eric Bubniak, owner of EBD Construction LLC · November 6, 2026

Quick answer: In Gainesville and Hall County, adding a simple kitchen island typically costs about $3,000 to $8,000 installed. A custom island with seating runs about $8,000 to $15,000, and an island with a sink or cooktop about $10,000 to $25,000+. You need about 42 inches of walkway around it, so most kitchens need to be at least 12 to 13 feet wide. How much room do I need for an island?

  • Walkways: at least 42 inches on working sides, 48 inches if two people cook or the fridge and dishwasher face the island. 36 inches is the bare minimum on a side nobody works at.
  • Island size: at least about 2 by 4 feet to be useful. A 3-by-6-foot island gives you prep space and room for two or three stools.
  • Kitchen width: two runs of 24-inch cabinets, a 2-foot island and two 42-inch aisles need about 13 feet wall to wall.
  • Seating: about 24 inches of length per stool, with 15 inches of knee space at counter height or 12 inches at bar height.

Tip: Tape the island's outline on the floor and live with it for a few days before you order cabinets. If the dishwasher door or fridge hits the tape, the island is too big.

Island or peninsula?

A peninsula attaches to your existing cabinets on one end, so it needs one less walkway and fits kitchens too narrow for an island. It's also cheaper, since it often ties into the existing countertop and wiring. An island gives you traffic flow on all sides and a natural gathering spot. If neither fits, opening a wall to the dining or living room often creates the space; see our wall removal guide.

Should the sink or cooktop go in the island?

  • Sink in the island: you face the room while you prep and wash up. It's simplest over a crawl space or basement, where the drain can run under the floor. On a slab, the concrete has to be cut.
  • Cooktop in the island: it needs a hood above or a downdraft vent, plus a gas line or 240-volt circuit, and splatter can reach the seating side.
  • Keep it clear: many homeowners leave the island as clean prep and seating space and keep the sink under a window.

Kitchen island FAQs

How big does my kitchen need to be for an island?

Plan for at least 42 inches of walkway on every working side of the island, 48 inches if two people cook, and an island at least about 2 by 4 feet. That usually means a kitchen at least 12 to 13 feet wide. If it's tighter, a peninsula or a narrow movable island often works better.

How much overhang do I need for island seating?

Allow about 24 inches of counter length per stool, and about 15 inches of knee space at a standard 36-inch counter height or 12 inches at a 42-inch bar height. A long overhang needs steel brackets or legs to support the countertop.

Can I add an island without replacing my cabinets?

Yes. A new island can be built to match or contrast with your existing cabinets, and painted or stained island cabinets are a popular accent. If the existing counters will be replaced anyway, it's a good time to add the island so the stone matches.

Is it worth putting the sink in the island?

It can be, if you like facing the room while you work, but moving a sink means new drain and vent piping through the floor, and it's easier over a crawl space or basement than on a slab. Expect about $1,000 to $4,000 for the plumbing on top of the island itself.

Do I need a permit to add a kitchen island?

A plain island without plumbing or wiring usually doesn't. Adding outlets, lights, a sink, a dishwasher or a cooktop does need electrical and plumbing permits and inspections. Eric pulls all permits himself.

How long does it take to add a kitchen island?

About 1 to 2 weeks of work for a simple island once cabinets arrive, and 2 to 3 weeks for an island with plumbing or a cooktop. Stock cabinets ship in days to a couple of weeks; semi-custom and custom take 4 to 10 weeks, and countertops are templated after the island is set.
