Privacy policy

Last updated October 9, 2026

What we collect

When you fill out our consultation form we receive your name, phone, email, address or town and the project details you type in. We use that only to contact you about your project. We do not sell it or share it with anyone outside EBD Construction LLC, except the form service that delivers it to us (Netlify).

Cookies, analytics and ads

This site uses Google Analytics and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, whether a form was sent or a phone number was tapped. These tools set cookies or similar technology in your browser.

We use remarketing with Google Ads and Meta. That means Google, Meta and other third-party vendors may show our ads to you on other websites and apps after you visit this site, based on your past visits. They use cookies to do this. We never send your form answers to these services.

Your choices

  • Opt out of personalized Google ads at Google Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • Manage Meta ads in your Facebook ad preferences.
  • Opt out of many third-party ad vendors at aboutads.info.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
