Two-story screened porch

An aging porch replaced with two levels of outdoor living: a screened porch with a timber-framed gable and an open deck above.

  1. Where it started

    The original porch was worn out and too small for how the family wanted to use the back of the house.

  2. A new structure

    We tore it down to the house and framed a new, stronger floor system.

  3. The gable goes up

    The timber-framed gable gives the screened porch its height and character.

  4. Railings and ceiling

    Cable railings keep the view open, and a tongue-and-groove ceiling finishes the space.

  5. Almost there

    Fans, lighting and trim go in before screening and stain.

  6. The finished porch

    Stained cedar, black gutters and screens make it feel like part of the house.
